Our Services

Nkosi Customs offers four core customisation services. Every job starts with a free consultation so we can recommend the right products for your vehicle, driving style and budget.

Custom Rims

We stock and fit a wide range of alloy and custom rims, from 15-inch daily fitments to large-diameter sport rims. Our team ensures correct offset and fitment for your specific make and model, protecting your suspension and bearings from unnecessary wear. Our 15″ custom rim range is priced at R7 000 each.

  • 15″ custom rims from R7 000 each
  • Wide range of sizes and finishes
  • Correct offset and fitment checks
  • Wheel balancing included

Tyres

We supply and fit tyres from leading brands, matched to your rim size, vehicle type and driving needs, whether that's daily commuting, performance driving, or off-road use. Our most popular range is the Vitour Neo Formula X, available in sizes from 165/40R15 to 175/50R15 and up.

  • Leading tyre brands in stock, including Vitour Neo Formula X
  • Prices range from R1 000 – R1 200 per tyre, depending on size
  • Wheel alignment and balancing
  • Advice on the right tyre for your use case

Suspension

From lowering springs to fully adjustable coilovers and air suspension systems, our technicians tailor your vehicle's ride height and handling to match your intended look and driving style. Suspension kits range from R900 for a basic set of lowering springs up to R15 000 for a full digitally-controlled air suspension setup.

  • Lowering springs from R900
  • Coilover kits (adjustable height and damping)
  • Air suspension installation up to R15 000
  • Alignment after every suspension job

Sound Systems

Our audio specialists design and install sound systems ranging from simple head-unit and speaker upgrades to fully custom, competition-grade installations with sound deadening and custom enclosures. Our standard Pioneer package (head unit, component speakers, subwoofers and dual amplifiers) is priced at R7 500, fitted.

  • Pioneer head units, speakers and amplifiers
  • Full package from R7 500, fitted
  • Custom subwoofer enclosures
  • Sound deadening installation
